Murmur

A calm logic puzzle about birds on a rooftop.

Every bird faces up, down, left or right. Tap one and it flies off the way it faces. Every bird in its path takes fright and flies off its own way, and those birds scare more birds. One good tap can clear half the roof.

Each level gives you only a few taps, exactly as many as you need. The puzzle is finding where to start.

  • 200 levels in five chapters, then Endless Sky, where the levels never run out
  • Hints and Undo, a star for clearing a level on your first try
  • Light and dark rooftops
  • Plays offline. No account and no sign-in: your progress stays on your phone.

Support

Something not working? Email support.murmur@gmail.com with your phone model and what happened. I read every message.

Lost progress after changing phones? Progress is saved only on the phone. It comes back only if Android backup restored the app's data.

Start again? Settings → Reset progress. Uninstalling also deletes everything Murmur saved.

Ad choices (Europe and the UK): Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Privacy and terms

Murmur has no accounts and sends me nothing. Ads come from Google AdMob and Unity Ads. Read the full privacy policy and the terms of service.
