Murmur Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Murmur is a puzzle game for Android made by Jayanthi Varadhan, an individual developer in India ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Murmur.

The short version

  • Murmur has no accounts and no sign-in. I don't run any servers, and I don't receive any personal information about you.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your phone.
  • Murmur shows ads from Google AdMob and Unity Ads. Their software inside the app collects some information about your device to show ads, count them and prevent fraud. Their own privacy policies apply to that information.

Information saved on your phone

Murmur saves your progress (levels cleared, stars, hints), your settings (sound, vibration, theme), the levels your phone has made, and how many ad videos you have watched today. This stays on your phone.

If Android backup is on, Android may back up your progress to your Google account; Google handles that backup and I can't see it. Uninstalling Murmur deletes this information from your phone. "Reset progress" in Settings deletes your progress.

Ads, and the information ad partners collect

Murmur uses these ad partners:

  • Google AdMob, from Google
  • Unity Ads, from Unity Technologies

When an ad is requested or shown, they may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • information about your device, such as its model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads (for example views and taps) and with the app;
  • diagnostic information about how the ads and the app perform.

They use it to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where the law allows and you agree, show ads based on your interests. The information is sent over encrypted connections.

Learn more:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

I don't sell your personal information, and I don't receive the information the ad partners collect.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Murmur asks for your consent the first time you open it, using Google's consent form. You can change your answer at any time in Settings, under "Ad privacy choices".
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ads personalisation, in your phone's settings (for example Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Murmur works offline. Without an internet connection no ads load and no information is sent.

Children

Murmur is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child's information has been collected through the ads, contact me and I will help you reach the ad partner.

Keeping and deleting information

I don't keep any information about you. Information on your phone is deleted when you uninstall Murmur. The ad partners keep the information they collect for the periods set out in their privacy policies; you can ask them to delete it through the links above, and you can reset your advertising ID at any time.

Security

Murmur doesn't send your game data anywhere. The ad partners send their information over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
